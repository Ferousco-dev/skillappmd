## What the stakeholder review actually caught

Recorded because a gate that never changes anything is theatre:

- **`DEC-018`** — the analyst had silently replaced BRIEF §38's `Unknown` rights state with
  all-false booleans. The user reversed it. "Known not to be redistributable" and "not known
  whether redistributable" have identical consequences today and entirely different ones
  tomorrow; collapsing them destroyed the distinction that makes the second resolvable. This is
  the exact fact/inference confusion `DOM-006` exists to prevent, introduced inside the model
  meant to prevent it.
- **`DEC-019`** — v1.0 retained full content for every occurrence unconditionally, including
  `unknown` licences. The user accepted the analyst's own flag and directed rights-aware,
  default-non-permanent retention. Largest residual legal exposure in v1.0, now closed.
- **`NFR-011`** — an invented number, demoted before it could gate anything.
- **`REQ-063`** — promoted to mandatory; removal architecture now exists from the start.
- **8 gaps** the analyst identified and the user approved as new requirements.
